Last reviewed: September 2026

Overview

KDS Airdrop Hub respects your privacy. This page explains the general approach to information and privacy on this website.

Information We Collect

KDS Airdrop Hub does not intentionally ask visitors to submit private wallet credentials, seed phrases, private keys, or passwords.

If a future feature collects information such as an email address, the purpose and relevant handling of that information will be explained at the point of collection.

Newsletter

The newsletter area of the website is currently presented as a non-active feature. No newsletter signup information is intentionally collected through the current placeholder interface.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use technologies provided by hosting, analytics, security, or other third-party services where applicable. Such services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Third-Party Websites

Some educational content may refer to external websites or blockchain projects. Once you leave KDS Airdrop Hub, the privacy practices of those external services are governed by their own policies.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to maintain the website, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features or data practices change. The latest review date is shown at the top of this page.
